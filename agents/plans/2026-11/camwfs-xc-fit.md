## Problem Statement

We need a version of pupilFit that uses cross-correlation.

## Discussion
<!-- Put more expository text here.  Fully describe the expected behavior and rationale. Include references to other parts of the software that serve as examples. Provide a link to an API/SDK manual.   -->

pupilFit finds circle edges by tresholding, and works well on modulated PWFS images which have filled circles.  When unmodulated this does not work.  We need a pupilXCFit app that does the same but uses cross correlation of dark subtracted pupil image with a reference.  

We can use mxlib's cross correlation by FFT, which includes masking and windowing (don't know yet if necessary).

We should have a toggle that selects between camwfs_ref and camwfs_fit_ref, which will be a separate reference image loaded just for this.  

We can modify alignLoop to enable switching between the output of camwfs-fit and camwfs-xc-fit when we switch modes.

With mxlib MFT refinement at 0.01 px, the full dark-subtracted stacks gave `(−0.04, +0.07)` px. The two target halves gave `(−0.03, +0.06)` and `(−0.04, +0.08)` px against the full reference. The recorded pair tests repeatability. Use injected shifts to measure accuracy.
- **Exploratory subpixel check.** In the dark-subtracted unmodulated reference and in `wfsref.fits`, inject `(2.50, −1.25)` and `(−1.50, 2.25)` px using cubic interpolation. ## Follow-up and Edge Cases
<!-- The agent should list any planned follow up and any edge cases that are not addressed -->

- Full-frame correlation estimates a common translation. If pupil illumination or geometry changes differently across quadrants, compare full-frame and per-pupil shifts in replay before deciding whether per-pupil weighting is needed. Diameter and circle-edge products are outside the control-stream requirement.
- A `camwfs_avg` frame published after a modulation change can still include exposures from the previous mode. Keep the alignment loop open through the averaging transition and verify that the first measurement used for correction comes from a full window in the new mode.
- A reference or corrector calibration change can move the loop zero even when both algorithms use the same sign and scale. Verify the reference, controller offsets and correction matrix together during open-loop commissioning.

## Implementation and Verification

- `pupilXCFit` uses normalized `imageXCorrFFT` with a default per-axis capture limit of 10 px and a minimum peak of 0.5. Square images use MFT refinement at 0.01 px for 120 × 120, with a wider grid spacing for smaller images to cover the nearest-pixel interval. Rectangular images use mxlib's interpolated FFT peak because its MFT requires square input. An invalidated or rejected sample puts two NaNs on the measurement stream so the consumer can clear its readiness.
- `telem_pupilxcfit` archives each processed live frame with its acquisition timestamp, the configured reference stream name, and the fit acceptance flag. Finite measured `dx`, `dy`, and normalized peak are included even for rejected fits. A frame without a finite result carries only the frame and reference metadata and the failed acceptance flag.
- `alignLoop` defaults to its primary stream. With an alternate configured, `input_source` selects `primary` or `alternate` with the loop open. Its state is Busy until the selected monitor supplies a valid measurement acquired after selection, then Ok. `loop_state` closure requests, including upstream requests, are held off until that measurement. Both sources use the existing interaction matrix, gains and offsets.
- The offline replay through the production fitter measured the dark-subtracted unmodulated pair at `(−0.04, +0.07)` px. With injections in `wfsref.fits`, the worst error against the known translations was 0.05 px per axis over the seven tested translations. For shifts of at most 2.5 px per axis, the largest difference from `pupilFitter` was 0.15 px. At the two ±5 px x shifts, `pupilFitter` measured +3.26 and −3.94 px in x, while cross-correlation measured +4.96 and −4.95 px. The legacy fit can underreport motion when a pupil reaches a quadrant edge.
- Both application binaries built. The `pupilXCFit`, `alignLoop` and legacy `pupilFit` Catch2 suites passed. An isolated ImageStreamIO smoke test exercised the running fitter monitors and frame grabber, including switching to the separately populated reference, emitting an invalidation, recovering on the next live frame, and preserving the original acquisition timestamp.
